Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The Club Owner is a single-player game made by Thomas Hutchinson. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

We don't collect your data

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Your saved games

Your saved games and any data packs you import are kept on your device, and in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) if you have backups switched on. We never receive them. If you delete the app, they are deleted from your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases, including buying the game itself, are handled entirely by Apple (on iPhone, through the App Store) or Google (on Android, through Google Play). We never see your payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so it can add the in-game money. Their handling of your information is covered by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Links

The game can open this website in your browser (for example to find data packs). This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the game's support page on GitHub.
