The Club Owner

Take over a struggling club at the foot of a five-tier football pyramid and build it into champions. You don't pick the team: you run the club.

  • Hire and fire the manager, and set the transfer and wage budgets.
  • Set ticket prices and keep the fans and the board on side.
  • Build your stadium stand by stand, and develop a youth academy.
  • Do the deals: shirt sponsors, stadium naming rights and cup prize money.
  • Climb the pyramid, with play-offs, domestic cups and continental competitions.

For iPhone and Android. Every club, league and cup is fictional; data packs let you rename them.
