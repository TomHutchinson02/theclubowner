Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Tiny Airline is a single-player game made by Thomas Hutchinson. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

We don't collect your data

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Your saved game

Your airline is saved only on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so it can unlock the feature. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
