Build an airline across Europe

Start with one nine-seat plane and an office in London. Buy landing rights, open routes, set fares and grow from island hops to jets between the capitals, while a rival airline tries to beat you to the best routes.

In the game

75 real European airports · 12 real aircraft, from small propeller planes to big jets · landing licences and runway upgrades · routes shared by several planes · fares that change how many people fly · breakdowns, storms, fuel prices and tourist booms · a tutorial to get you started.
